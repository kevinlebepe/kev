# NextQ website remake: brief (parked)

Saved on 7 October 2026 for later. Nothing is built yet.

## The idea

Remake the NextQ website in the spirit of the reference screenshots in `website-reference/` (a documentation product site). We take the structure and the calm, white, roomy feel. We do not copy its text, illustrations or branding. The NextQ mascot carries the personality in place of their stock people.

## What we take from the reference

1. A small grey kicker above each large, bold headline, for example "Online registers for every door" above "Sign in here. Sign in now."
2. One clear blue call to action per section, for example "Create your first register".
3. A tablet in the hero, straight on, with soft coloured lines running behind it like lines on a register page.
4. A row of four short promise cards under the hero.
5. Tabs that switch between uses, each with a text column and a screen.
6. Three feature columns with a short title and two lines of text.
7. A grey band near the end: "Start from R 350. Grow when you need to."
8. Plenty of white space, no clutter.

## The NextQ version, section by section

1. **Header:** NextQ logo, Product, Uses, Pricing, Contact, then Log in and a "Get started" button.
2. **Hero:**
   - kicker: "Online registers, no paper"
   - headline: "Sign in here. Sign in now."
   - button: "Create your first register"
   - a tablet showing the conference visitor form, with the mascot peeking over its top edge
3. **Four promise cards:**
   - "Your data in South Africa, POPIA aware"
   - "Works offline"
   - "Confirmations by email"
   - "PDF and Excel registers"
4. **About:** "So much more than a sign in sheet. Welcome to NextQ." with one short paragraph.
5. **Uses tabs:** Conferences and events, Staff transport, Visitors and reception, Exam rooms and training. Each tab has a short text, a "Get started" link and a screen from the app.
6. **Features:**
   - Registers and forms
   - Live view
   - Security at the gate
   - Incidents
   - Invoicing
   - Certificates
   Three per row, each with a mascot pose in place of a stock icon.
7. **How a visit goes:** the four panel mascot strip from poster design 10.
8. **Pricing band:**
   - R 350 once off for a single register
   - a monthly subscription
   - "Contact us" for a customised registry
9. **Contact and footer:**

   - Pretoria and Johannesburg, South Africa
   - "NextQ, a product of Bakwena Industries"

## Rules carried over from the posters

1. Solid colours only, no gradients:
   - cream #fff4dc
   - navy #1e2150
   - mascot purple #6670b5
   - green #12a88a
   - yellow #ffd25e
   - coral #ff7a59
   - mint #cfeadb
2. Fonts from `fonts/`: Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, Plus Jakarta Sans for text, Fraunces for the odd accent.
3. The mascot appears alone, without flowers or leaves. Poses are in `m-front.png`, `m-back.png`, `m-side.png` and `m-jump.png`. A vector version would be better for large sizes.
4. No dashes in the copy.

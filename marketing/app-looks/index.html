Pro look: ten ideas for the tablet

Each idea shows the home screen and the two confirmation screens: signed in and signed out. Everything moves as it would on the tablet. Pick a number and I will build it into the app.

1. Cloud White2. Footer Band3. Icon Tiles4. Peek Over5. Stacked Rows6. Centre Stage7. Soft Press8. Boarding Pass9. Big Words10. Night Desk

1Cloud White

Two white squares on a soft blue grey, speech bubbles and pill buttons. The mascot breathes and the bubbles pop in turn.

2Footer Band

White squares with the button in a coloured band along the bottom. The arrow in each button nudges forward.

3Icon Tiles

A coloured rounded tile inside each white square holds the mascot, like an app icon. The tiles gently bob.

4Peek Over

Thin outlined white squares on a dotted grid. The mascot peeks over the top edge of each square and ducks back.

5Stacked Rows

Two wide white rows, one above the other. Easy to reach on a phone held upright. The mascot slides in on each row.

6Centre Stage

One large mascot in the middle between two white squares. It leans toward each door in turn.

7Soft Press

Raised white squares on light grey that look pressable. The buttons breathe with a soft glow.

8Boarding Pass

White squares cut like tickets, with a dashed tear line. The mascot waves from the stub.

9Big Words

Very white and calm: large words, a small mascot in the corner, and an arrow that slides.

10Night Desk

White squares on deep navy for evenings and dark gates. The mascot glows softly.

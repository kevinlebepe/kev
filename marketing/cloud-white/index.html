Cloud White: five variations

The moving mascot in the Cloud White look, without the small mascot beside This trip. Each variation shows the home screen and the signed in and signed out confirmations. Pick one, or mix parts, and I will build it into the app.

1. Classic2. Tinted3. Side by side4. On a cloud5. Big buttons

1Classic

Two white squares on soft blue grey. Greeting bubbles in the corners. The mascot waves hello on Sign in and grins on Sign out.

2Tinted

The same white squares with a faint blue and a faint orange wash, and the bubble speaking from the mascot.

3Side by side

The mascot stands to the left of the words in each square. Bigger words, more room on a wide tablet.

4On a cloud

Soft white clouds along the bottom of the screen. Each mascot stands on its own little cloud.

5Big buttons

A smaller mascot on top and a button as wide as the square, the easiest to tap with a thumb.
